Dino Truck Smash!

For grown-ups: tap anywhere (or press any key) to jump, and each jump plays the next note of Twinkle Twinkle. Hold a finger down for turbo. The truck squashes cars, bursts gift boxes and pops balloons all by itself. Ramps do flips, mud makes the truck messy (rainbow button washes it), and every 8 stars brings fireworks and a new time of day. Green button makes Dino roar, yellow button honks, rainbow button changes colour. Nothing can go wrong.
